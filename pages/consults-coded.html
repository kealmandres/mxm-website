Culture Reboot & Leadership Mandates

The one thing we can't augment (yet) is atmosphere. Artificial Intelligence is not a substitute emotional intelligence - people are the hardest part of any business, while also the most crucial. The culture of a team is synonymous with its cohesion. Over the last 8 years our founder has handled staff and clients / customers in; hospitality, marketing, talent and tech industries, she is particularly passionate about helping founders and senior staff leading with authority and empathy. Effective leadership commands respect without instilling fear. The state of work has changed drastically since 2020 - socioculturally, morale is at an all time low. For almost 5 years now we have had; mortality, cost of living crises, and geopolitical unrest front of mind - the perfect recipe for collective burnout, with studies to prove it. MxM offers culture reboots with a customised leadership mandate for sustained team health. Just like a well-oiled machine, the whole is greater than the sum of its parts.

  • Culture assessment and design
  • Leadership development
  • Change management
  • Team performance optimization
